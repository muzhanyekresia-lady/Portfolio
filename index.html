Safe Nest — Childcare Trust Marketplace

UX Research, Trust Architecture & Product Design Case Study

The Core Problem: Trust, Not Usability

Parents need a safe and trustworthy way to hire childcare online, but trusting a stranger with their child feels risky. Traditional childcare platforms fail because they treat childcare as a quick transaction. Safe Nest treats childcare as a psychological risk decision.

User Persona: Sarah (Age 34, Marketing Manager)

Sarah works full-time and struggles to balance work and parenting. When interviewed, Sarah noted: “My biggest fear is leaving my child with someone who isn't trustworthy. I'd want background checks, video introductions, and references from families like mine.”

The 5 Trust Pillars of Safe Nest

  • Trust-First Design: Rigorous verification before visibility — only approved caregivers appear in search.
  • Humanized Profiles: Video intros from caregivers displaying caregiving philosophy.
  • Trial Bookings: Short, low-pressure sessions before long-term commitment.
  • Community Trust Signals: Verified parent reviews and reference validation.
  • Gradual Commitment Model: Discovery → Trial Session → Long-term Hire.
